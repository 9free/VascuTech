Our Work

Two products, one goal

An AI training app and a vein-imaging device, both built around the same idea: real, immediate feedback for vascular access.

The app

VascuAI

An AI-guided training app for IV cannulation, currently in closed preview with our first cohort of nursing and medical students.

Clinical Chatbot

Ask evidence-based questions about IV insertion, complications, and technique — answered from curated clinical references.

Training Validator

Record or upload a practice attempt and get phase-by-phase feedback on technique.

Learning Hub

Vein anatomy guides, clinical guidelines, and a self-assessment quiz.

Scenario Library

Practice across risk-tiered patient scenarios, from a practice mannequin to difficult-access adults.

Session History

Review past chats and practice sessions in one place.

Always Available

A clinical question doesn't wait for the next rotation — and neither does VascuAI.

The hardware

DUALVein

A portable vein-imaging device, paired with the same software behind VascuAI.

Concept in development

NIR Vein Visualizer

Highlights subsurface vein position in real time using near-infrared imaging.

Direct-Skin Projection

Projects the vein pattern directly onto the patient's skin — no separate screen to look away to.

Companion Software

Pairs with the same segmentation technology used across VascuTech's products.
